# Spec 011a — questions

## From the tester (acceptance tests, branch `tests/011a-partner-on-stock-documents`)

None of these blocked the tests. Each states what the tests do, so the architect only has to confirm or correct.
The branch is cut from `tests/011-default-warehouse-stock-balance` (no implementation of 011 on it), so the
tests of spec 011 fail here too until the builder's 011 is merged; "Balanced" (AC-90) uses the routes of 011.

### T-Q1. Changes to earlier tests (AC-01)
Made on this branch only:
- Item 2 — `McpToolListTests`: `partnerId` in the pinned input properties of `stock_document_create`,
  `stock_document_update` and `stock_document_list`; `required` of all three is unchanged.
- Item 3 — the helper `Stock.Replacement` (and through it `Units.Replacement` and
  `OrderApi.DocumentReplacement`) sends `"partnerId": null`; the two hand-written replace bodies
  (`StockDocumentDraftTests` 005/AC-23, `McpStockToolTests` 005/AC-82) gain it; `StockDocumentDraftTests`
  005/AC-24 ("replace omitting a property") gains the row `partnerId` (the case of AC-22).
- Item 1 — nothing had to change: no earlier test pins the **exact** property set of a stock document, of
  its summary or of an `outputSchema`. The new property is asserted by the new tests.
No test that asserts a quantity, a number, a posting result, an order's progress or the tool list changed.

### T-Q2. Item 3 and documents that are not a receipt or an issue
`Stock.TransferReplacement` does **not** send `partnerId` (unchanged requests). But earlier tests that replace
a **count**, or a transfer draft in the "missing `toWarehouseId`" cases, do it through `Stock.Replacement`
and therefore now send `"partnerId": null` as well. R1 / AC-26 accept that, so no expectation changed; it is
outside the letter of item 3 ("for a receipt or an issue"). Say so if those requests must stay as they were
and I will give them a helper of their own. Omission on a transfer and a count is tested by 011a/AC-26.

### T-Q3. Keys the spec does not spell out
**Tested:**
- AC-27, a line with `partnerId`: only `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` (no key asserted), also for `"partnerId": null`
  on the line.
- AC-34, `POST …/reverse` with `partnerId` in the body: `400` with key `partnerId`, also for `null` (an
  unknown property whatever its value).
- AC-10: a malformed filter value is tested with `not-a-uuid`, `123` and a truncated uuid; an **empty**
  `?partnerId=` is not tested.

### T-Q4. R7 — combinations of header references (beyond AC-25)
**Tested on create:** unknown partner + inactive warehouse -> `REFERENCE_NOT_FOUND` with `partnerId` alone
(E2); unknown partner + unknown warehouse -> one `REFERENCE_NOT_FOUND` with both keys; inactive partner +
inactive warehouse -> one `REFERENCE_INACTIVE` with both keys; an inactive partner without the role ->
`REFERENCE_INACTIVE` (activity before role); a partner without the role + an unknown article and a service
article on the lines -> `PARTNER_ROLE_MISSING` alone (role before lines); a line quantity of 0 + an unknown
partner -> `400` for the line alone. In every case `errors` has **exactly** the keys named.

### T-Q5. R12 at posting (beyond AC-32, AC-33)
**Tested:** warehouse, partner and an article inactive -> exactly `warehouseId` and `partnerId`; then the
partner alone; then `lines[0].articleId`. A partner that is inactive **and** lost the role ->
`REFERENCE_INACTIVE`, and after reactivation `PARTNER_ROLE_MISSING`. After the role is given back the posting
succeeds with the partner. "No number consumed" is tested by the number the document gets once it posts.

### T-Q6. R9 — "the value is only compared" (beyond AC-42, AC-43)
**Tested:** on a linked document a partner without the role, an inactive partner and `BOTH` all give
`ORDER_MISMATCH` with exactly `partnerId`; another partner + `W2` + the article of another order line gives
one `ORDER_MISMATCH` with exactly `partnerId`, `warehouseId`, `lines[0].articleId`; a **malformed**
`partnerId` on a linked document is `400` (validation comes first). The same holds on replace of a linked
draft (other partner, random uuid, other partner + `W2`).

### T-Q7. The mirror
AC-24, AC-31, AC-33 to AC-35 and AC-40 to AC-45 are written once against `OrderApi` and run for receipt /
purchase order / supplier and for issue / sales order / customer (`ReceiptPartnerTests`,
`IssuePartnerTests`), although the criteria name the receipt side only (AC-41 names the mirror of AC-40).

### T-Q8. Assumptions about masters
- AC-33, AC-35, AC-61: a role is taken away from a partner that a stock document (draft or posted) names, and
  the partner is deactivated and re-coded — R15 says this is allowed; the tests expect `200`.
- AC-28: renaming / re-coding the partner changes `partner.code` and `partner.name` on the documents and
  leaves their `updatedAt` as it was.
- AC-44 also expects that an **unlinked** receipt for the order's supplier, deactivated since, is still
  refused with `REFERENCE_INACTIVE` — the exemption of R10 belongs to the link.

### T-Q9. MCP (section 5)
**Tested:** `partnerId` is in the input properties of the three tools with a description, allows a string
(and `null` on create and update) and is not in `required`; the `outputSchema` of `stock_document_get`,
`_create`, `_update`, `_post`, `_reverse` has the property `partner`, and that of `stock_document_list`
contains `"partner"` somewhere (the summary schema). Descriptions: `partnerId` in the three tools,
`isSupplier` and `isCustomer` in `stock_document_create`, `PARTNER_ROLE_MISSING` in create, update and post,
`IN_USE` and the words "stock document" in `partner_delete`. `stock_document_reverse` with `partnerId` ->
`VALIDATION_FAILED` with key `partnerId`.

### T-Q10. Not covered by these tests
AC-01 (manual); AC-02 (the migration, R17) and AC-03 (builder). Documents that existed before the migration
cannot be produced through the public surface.

## Answers from the architect to the tester (2026-10-10)

Every reading is confirmed. **No answer changes a test.** Four answers amend the spec in words (T-Q2, T-Q3,
T-Q7, T-Q9); none of them changes a rule.

### T-Q1
Confirmed. Sending `"partnerId": null` from `Stock.Replacement`, and through it from `Units.Replacement` and
`OrderApi.DocumentReplacement`, is item 3 done where it belongs; on a linked draft `null` keeps the order's
partner (R8), so those tests are unaffected. Item 1 having nothing to change is fine.

### T-Q2
Leave it as it is; no helper of its own. `"partnerId": null` on a count or a transfer is accepted and means
nothing (R1, E9, AC-26), so those requests assert what they asserted before. AC-01 item 3 now allows it: a
shared replace helper may send `"partnerId": null` for every type.

### T-Q3
- AC-27: `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` without a pinned key is right; the key of an unknown line property is the
  inherited one and this spec adds nothing to it. `null` makes no difference — the property is unknown.
- AC-34: confirmed, key `partnerId`, whatever the value (001/R10, 006/E9). R14 and AC-34 now name the key.
- An empty `?partnerId=` is 001/R15: a parameter with an empty value is treated as absent, so the list is
  unfiltered (`200`). R16 now says so. No test is required.

### T-Q4
All six confirmed; that is R7 stage by stage — validation, then not-found (header keys together), then
inactive (header keys together), then the role, then the lines — and each stage answers alone, so `errors`
has exactly the keys you name.

### T-Q5
Confirmed. Warehouse and partner are the header masters and are reported together and alone (005/R13); the
articles come after them; the role comes after every activity check (R12). "Inactive and lost the role" is
`REFERENCE_INACTIVE` first and `PARTNER_ROLE_MISSING` once reactivated. Reading "no number consumed" from the
number the document gets when it finally posts is right.

### T-Q6
Confirmed. On a linked document the value is only compared (R9): whatever the other partner is — without the
role, inactive, `BOTH`, unknown — the answer is `ORDER_MISMATCH` with `partnerId`, together with
`warehouseId` and `lines[i].articleId` when those differ too. A malformed value never reaches the comparison
(`400`). The same on replace.

### T-Q7
Confirmed, and wanted: the rules are written for "a receipt or an issue" and "the purchase order's supplier /
the sales order's customer" throughout; the criteria name the receipt side for brevity. Section 10 now says
that AC-24, AC-31, AC-33 to AC-35 and AC-40 to AC-45 hold mirrored.

### T-Q8
- Confirmed: R15 lets the partner be renamed, re-coded, deactivated and its roles changed while documents
  name it (004/R15 still asks for one role). `200` is right.
- Confirmed: renaming or re-coding the partner writes no document; `partner` shows the current code and name
  (R13, 005/R23) and the documents' `updatedAt` stays.
- Confirmed: the exemption of R10 belongs to the link. An unlinked receipt that newly names a deactivated
  partner is `REFERENCE_INACTIVE` (R5), whether or not a confirmed order has the same partner.

### T-Q9
Confirmed, all of it. Section 5 now says that the description of `partner_delete` names the stock document
as a further `IN_USE` case in those words. Keep the description assertions to "contains".

### T-Q10
Confirmed. AC-02 and AC-03 are the builder's.
