### T-Q3. R9: order of checks on posting, as tested
One draft count that is at once outdated, not convertible and has an inactive article is posted three times:
`REFERENCE_INACTIVE` -> (article reactivated) `QUANTITY_NOT_CONVERTIBLE` -> (factor restored) `COUNT_OUTDATED`.

### T-Q4. R10: an outdated count whose counted quantity equals the stock of now
Book 100, counted 97, then an issue of 3. Posting would write no entry and stock already equals the count,
but stock (97) differs from the book quantity (100). **Tested:** `COUNT_OUTDATED`; after a save the line shows
difference 0 and the count posts without entries. Is that right, or should it post?

### T-Q5. Reversal and the book quantity
- A reversal is never judged "outdated": a count of 97 (book 100), then an issue of 50, then the reversal ->
  `201`, stock 50.
- The reversing document's `lines` are compared with the original's as whole JSON values (R17: copies,
  including `bookQuantity` and `differenceQuantity`), so its `differenceQuantity` has the original's sign
  while its entry has the opposite one (AC-60).

### T-Q6. Readings the tests take where a criterion leaves room
- AC-22: for the repeated article the keys asserted are those of the criterion; other keys are allowed.
  For `COUNT_OUTDATED` and for `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` on a reversal the key set is asserted as **exact**.
- AC-37: six lines — in boxes (shortage), surplus, no difference, zero, a rounded conversion
  (`3.5 pack × 0.333333 = 1.166666`), an article never received (`0.000001`).
- AC-45 runs ten rounds with fresh tenants and accepts either of the two outcomes; nothing else.
- AC-46: also the next count is `SC-000002`.
- AC-80: the description of `stock_document_create` must contain "count" (any case) and that of
  `stock_document_post` must contain `COUNT_OUTDATED`. This is the only assertion on description text in the
  suite, because the criterion asks for it.
- AC-92 runs for X's draft count and for X's posted count, over HTTP and through tools.
- 005 `IN_USE` on a frozen `type` is asserted without a key (as the 005 tests do).
