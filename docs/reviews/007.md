**Per-tenant lock (open item 005/2, ADR-0012 amendment, condition 1).** `ArticleUnitOperations.SetAsync` and
`DeleteAsync` run wholly inside `IXerpDb.SerializedPerTenantAsync`, as do `PUT /articles/{id}` and now
`DELETE /articles/{id}`; every stock document write already did. So: two Sets of one new conversion cannot
both create it (E3, tested); a conversion cannot disappear between a draft's check and its save, nor a draft
be saved in a unit being deleted (R8, tested as a race accepting either outcome); a factor cannot change
between the read in posting and the save. The delete of a unit of measure takes no lock and needs none: the
restricting foreign keys decide, and both sides translate the violation. Nothing slow runs inside the lock.
