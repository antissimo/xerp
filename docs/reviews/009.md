**Before-MVP open items for 009 — all three done** (B-Q5; `open-items.md` updated).
- 005/3: `DocumentSeries` (key and prefix) replaces the stock document type in `DocumentCounter.Start` and
  `DocumentNumber.Format`; `purchaseOrder` / `PO` is a series, no order kind was added to
  `StockDocumentType`.
- 004/4: `partner_delete` answers `IN_USE` while an order names the partner — checked first, and again when

- 005/2: create, replace, delete, confirm, close and reopen of an order run wholly inside
  `SerializedPerTenantAsync`; posting and reversal read and write the order inside the same lock. So an
  order cannot be closed between a posting's check and its save, a conversion cannot be deleted while a
  draft order that uses it is being saved, and a factor cannot change between the read in a confirmation and
  its save. The deletes of a partner, a warehouse and a unit take no lock and need none: the restricting
  foreign keys decide and both sides translate the violation.

## Non-blocking

1. **The order list loads every line of the orders on the page** to compute `lineCount`, `totalAmount` and
   `receiptStatus` (up to 200 × 200 lines). The values are right; `total` and paging are decided in SQL.
   Performance only; open item 009/1, after MVP.
2. **`GET /purchase-orders/{id}` of a draft** reads the order and then its masters and current factors in
   separate statements, outside the lock — the shape of open item 007/1 (a replace of the draft plus the
   delete of a conversion it named, both in between, would end the read as `500`). A read only; recorded on
   007/1, after MVP.
3. Order lines are judged with `StockDocumentType.Receipt` passed to `StockLineChecks.References` and
   `Convert`. Correct (a quantity above zero that converts); when spec 010 makes the operations shared, name
   that argument for what it decides rather than for a stock document type.
4. The DbContext guard lets any code change the received quantity of a line of a confirmed order. By design
   (it is progress, B-Q1) and only `Order.Fulfil` / `TakeBack` do it; a database-level guard belongs with
   open item 005/4.
